When something goes wrong
Late, declined, not reached, not acknowledged or needing a new owner: CareLoop creates a task with a named person and a deadline, then escalates until a person acts. Each one counts towards unresolved results and reasons for delay. Screen 13 shows how these states look.

Where AI helps
AI writes, people decide. Three short texts for staff, each labelled as written by AI: a briefing on the worklist, a summary of the figures on the Impact page, and a draft of a message to the patient, which is never sent. AI never sets urgency, sends a message or closes a case.

The same ten stages work for breast, bowel, cervical and lung screening and for heart, kidney and diabetes checks. Each programme only changes its settings on screen 4: steps, deadlines and required evidence.
