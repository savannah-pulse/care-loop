Where AI helps
AI drafts, people decide. Plain-language explanations and Arabic translations for patients, checked by a clinician. Handoff summaries, approved by the referring clinician. Impact summaries, approved by the programme coordinator. AI never sets urgency, sends a message or closes a case.

The same ten stages work for breast, bowel, cervical and lung screening and for heart, kidney and diabetes checks. Each programme only changes its settings on screen 4: steps, deadlines and required evidence.
